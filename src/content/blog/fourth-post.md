Travelling has given me memories that stay with me long after I have come home. Kolkata felt full of energy and colour, while Budapest and Barcelona each had their own rhythm and character that made me want to keep exploring.

Berlin and Cologne were different again, and I enjoyed noticing how each city tells its story through its buildings, streets, and people. I do not think I travel only to see famous places; I like the feeling of being somewhere unfamiliar and coming back with a slightly wider view of the world.

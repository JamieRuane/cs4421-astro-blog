I have always liked blogs because they feel more personal than most of the internet. A good blog gives me a chance to follow someone's ideas, interests, and everyday experiences over time, whether they are writing about technology, travel, or something completely unexpected.

I also enjoy creating blogs because writing helps me organise my own thoughts. Building this site gives me a place to share what I am learning and to look back at how my interests develop. It is a small project, but that is part of what makes it enjoyable.
